The Mobile AI Literacy Initiative.

In the remote valleys of the Himalayas, access to modern technology and AI education is limited. Cyberscout changes that paradigm.

We deploy "Tech-Nomads"—experts backpacking with modern high-speed ultra-smart tablets, portable wifi dongles, and power banks, all transported seamlessly on robust Electric Unicycles (EUCs). We bring the classroom of the future directly to remote schools.

What We Do.

Equipping the next generation with the tools to navigate and build the future.

Sustainable Travel

Navigating rough mountain terrain using high-performance Electric Unicycles, minimizing our carbon footprint while maximizing reach.

Hands-on AI

Interactive sessions exploring ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and more. Starting with fun, playful concepts, before gradually advancing to deeper programming and design skills using portable wifi setups.

Boosting Imagination

Supercharging creative writing, art, and storytelling. We turn technology into an active canvas to give students a massive boost to their imagination and curiosity.
